# Problem

Incident operators cannot reliably reuse prior operational outcomes across fresh response sessions when history is disconnected from the decision path.

MERCURY addresses the structural gap between storing incident notes and deterministically changing a later action because a matching prior action failed. The primary users are incident commanders and operators; judges and auditors need independent proof.

Existing runbooks preserve advice but do not prove cross-process retrieval or expose exactly how evidence changed a decision. MERCURY does not execute production infrastructure, replace observability systems, or claim production recovery metrics.
